# Marketing Campaign Performance Analytics | Power BI

A Power BI marketing analytics portfolio project exploring campaign revenue, marketing spend, conversions, channel and platform performance, customer acquisition, and return on investment (ROI) using a synthetic practice dataset.

## Project Overview

This project presents a five-page interactive Power BI report designed to help marketing stakeholders review campaign performance and compare results across channels, platforms, customer segments, and targets. It demonstrates dashboard design, KPI reporting, data analysis, and business storytelling using Power BI and Excel.

> **Dataset note:** This is a synthetic/practice dataset for learning and portfolio demonstration. The results should not be interpreted as actual performance for a real business.

## Selected Insights

- **Meta Ads** has the highest channel revenue shown in the report, approximately **171.51M**, with reported ROAS of **114.09**.
- **Summer Sale 20** appears at the top of the displayed campaign revenue ranking at approximately **37M**.
- Returning customers contribute approximately **676.37M** in reported revenue, compared with **655.55M** from new customers and **168.70M** from VIP customers.
- Paid acquisition contributes approximately **0.63bn** in revenue, followed by organic acquisition at approximately **0.42bn**.
- The dashboard reports a **10.36% conversion rate** and **4.51% CTR**.

These observations summarize the supplied dashboard. They do not establish causation or prove that one channel is more cost-effective without validating metric definitions and comparable spend and target calculations.
